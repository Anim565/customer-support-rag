# AI Use Disclosure

## Project

Development of a Retrieval-Augmented Customer Support Framework
with Intelligent Escalation: A Diagnostic Laboratory Case Study.

MSc Applied Machine Learning
Strathmore University
DSA 8401 Capstone Project
November 2026

## 1. Generative AI tools used

ChatGPT was used extensively as a research, technical learning,
programming and documentation assistant during this project.

Language models accessed through the Groq API were also used
within the experimental and deployed research framework.

These uses are distinguished below.

## 2. AI assistance during research development

ChatGPT assisted with:

- Comparing alternative methodological approaches.
- Research architecture and workflow design.
- Python script and notebook code generation.
- Software debugging and error resolution.
- Data preparation and preprocessing procedures.
- Synthetic enquiry design and generation.
- Structured extraction and prompt engineering.
- Feature engineering.
- Model development and evaluation procedures.
- Error analysis and interpretation of experimental results.
- Evidence verification and response validation design.
- API and frontend implementation.
- Docker configuration and deployment troubleshooting.
- Repository organization and reproducibility documentation.

AI assistance contributed to the generation
and revision of research implementation code.

## 3. AI within the research framework

Language models form part of the proposed customer support
architecture.

The first language model component performs structured
customer enquiry extraction, including test identification,
intent recognition and requested information extraction.

The second component performs structured response generation
after supervised classification and deterministic evidence
verification permit answering.

AI-assisted extraction and retrieval also supported the
preparation of evidence annotation workbooks.

The final research system does not rely exclusively on an
LLM to determine whether sufficient evidence is available.

## 4. Researcher involvement and verification

The researcher remained responsible for the research problem,
laboratory domain requirements, methodological decisions,
execution of the research workflow and interpretation of
results.

AI-assisted code was executed and iteratively tested within
the project environment.

Experimental metrics were obtained from executed research
workflows rather than accepted solely from generated text.

Observed failures, unsuccessful experiments, limitations
and unmet acceptance criteria were retained and reported.

Human review formed part of the privacy and evidence
annotation procedures.

AI-generated suggestions were not treated as inherently
correct but independently validated.

## 5. Data protection

Historical laboratory enquiries contain potentially
sensitive information.

The research incorporated anonymization, de-identification,
privacy approval and restricted local data storage.

Privacy-approved text was used for the external language
model extraction workflow.

These controls reduce privacy risk but do not establish that
all historical processing was independently audited or that
re-identification risk has been eliminated.

API credentials, raw customer conversations and restricted
research datasets are excluded from source-code submission.

## 6. Limitations of AI assistance

Generative AI may produce incorrect code, inaccurate
technical explanations, fabricated references or unsupported
methodological recommendations.

The researcher is responsible for checking AI-assisted
outputs against executed experiments, available evidence
and applicable academic requirements.

Language model outputs are also affected by external service
availability, model variability and rate limits.

The research reports these limitations where they affect
the implemented framework.

## 7. Academic responsibility

The researcher accepts responsibility for the accuracy,
integrity and presentation of the submitted research.

This disclosure describes the use of AI
assistance throughout the project.

The disclosure should be read together with the applicable
university and course academic integrity requirements.
